## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Reconciliation exists only because firms are technologically backward. | oversimplification | Acknowledge that distributed legal entities, counterparties, and control needs create real pluralism even in better architectures |
| RISK-002 | A single platform can fully eliminate reconciliation. | overstatement | Keep the later architecture thesis disciplined; the point is to reduce hidden comparison burden, not promise universal elimination |
| RISK-003 | Reconciliation is only a back-office accounting issue. | inaccurate reduction | Show its presence across payments, collateral, service workflows, post-trade, and reporting |
| RISK-004 | The system survives only because reconciliation teams heroically patch broken infrastructure. | melodrama | Emphasize structural dependence and real operational discipline without collapsing into catastrophe language |
