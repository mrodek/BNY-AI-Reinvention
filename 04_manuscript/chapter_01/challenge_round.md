## What Now Works Better

- The chapter no longer competes with CH02 and CH03 for explanatory ownership.

## Remaining Vulnerabilities

- The phrase "the institutions global finance cannot function without" is strong. It is probably acceptable in context, but it still invites scrutiny if a reader wants a more explicit explanation of what kind of failure or dependency is being implied.
- The sentence about BNY being "one of the most interesting companies in global finance" is rhetorically effective, but it is also more subjective than the surrounding prose.
- The chapter is intentionally light on mechanics now. That is the correct structural decision, but it increases the importance of CH02 and CH03 landing quickly and clearly.

## Strongest Challenge

- Challenge: this is still closer to an opening essay than to a standalone analytical chapter.
- Severity: medium
- Why it does not break the chapter: at this point that is a design choice, not an accident. The chapter now earns its overture role because the next two chapters clearly absorb the explanatory burden.

## Recommended Minor Revisions

1. Consider softening "cannot function without" to slightly more precise language such as "depend on for core infrastructure functions" if you want to reduce the chance of overstatement.
2. Consider changing "one of the most interesting companies in global finance" to something slightly less subjective, such as "one of the most strategically important companies in global finance," if you want the tone to stay more analytic.
3. Keep the current ending structure. It is now doing exactly the right handoff work.

Proceed. Chapter 1 is now in good enough shape to serve as the opening of the book, with only optional wording refinements remaining.
