## Drafting Guidance

- Keep the prose readable and workflow-first.
- Distinguish execution, clearing, netting, depository activity, and settlement clearly.
- Use concrete pressure points such as affirmation, margin, liquidity, funding readiness, and fails to keep the chapter operator-facing.
- Avoid overclaiming BNY-specific internal fragmentation; use the sources to support the general burden of the business and BNY's role inside it.
