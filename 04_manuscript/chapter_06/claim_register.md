# Claim Register: CH06

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | Clearing and settlement are distinct but tightly connected layers that turn executed trades into completed obligations, final movement of cash and securities, and durable records. | descriptive synthesis | supported | SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-006, SRC-008 | Core definitional claim for the chapter |
| CLM-002 | Clearing is not just administrative matching; it includes netting, counterparty-risk reduction, and the transformation of large numbers of bilateral obligations into more governable exposures. | descriptive interpretation | supported | SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-007, SRC-008 | Important distinction for broad readers |

| CLM-004 | Clearing and settlement are strong businesses because markets need reliable post-trade completion, risk management, and infrastructure continuity whether markets are calm or stressed. | strategic interpretation | supported | SRC-004, SRC-008, SRC-009 | Business-importance claim |
| CLM-005 | The polished external smoothness of clearing and settlement depends on timing discipline, liquidity coordination, risk controls, and exception handling beneath the surface. | interpretive | supported | SRC-011, SRC-012, SRC-013, SRC-014, SRC-015 | Now supported more directly through affirmation, netting, funding, and formal risk-management sources |
| CLM-006 | Faster settlement and tighter post-trade deadlines expose how dependent the system is on affirmation quality, netting, financing readiness, and operational coordination. | descriptive interpretation | supported | SRC-013, SRC-014 | Useful bridge into workflow friction and later AI-native discussion |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Clearing and settlement are just back-office administration. | misleading | Show the risk, liquidity, and completion functions clearly |
| RISK-002 | Public materials prove exact BNY internal fragmentation in clearing and settlement. | weak evidence | Frame internal burden as careful inference unless stronger sources are added |
| RISK-003 | All post-trade markets work through one identical clearing and settlement model. | overgeneralization | Distinguish equities, fixed income, and product-specific variation where needed |
| RISK-004 | Settlement completion means all downstream record, custody, and reporting work is finished. | misleading | Make the handoff to safekeeping, records, and later control work explicit |
