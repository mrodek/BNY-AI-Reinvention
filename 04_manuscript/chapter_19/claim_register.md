# Claim Register: CH19

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | AI-native asset servicing should be framed as a continuously supervised operating model rather than as fully autonomous fund administration. | strategic framing | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-004 | Core guardrail for the chapter |
| CLM-002 | The clearest early gains in AI-native asset servicing are AI-powered NAV oversight, anomaly detection, reconciliation triage, and faster context assembly across the servicing stack. | workflow claim | supported | SRC-001, SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-007 | Main business-line transformation claim |
| CLM-003 | Integrated data, real-time visibility, and automated oversight are prerequisites for useful AI in servicing because the business depends on explaining and correcting recurring breaks across records, cash, valuations, and investor activity. | architecture-to-business synthesis | supported | SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-007, SRC-008 | Direct bridge from Part IV into CH19 |
| CLM-004 | Human supervision should remain strongest where workflows affect official records, client-facing outputs, regulatory reporting, or consequential adjustments, even as lower-consequence triage and monitoring become more autonomous. | control-boundary claim | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-007 | Important to avoid overclaiming autonomy |
| CLM-005 | AI-native asset servicing should improve transparency and control evidence for operators and clients, not only reduce manual effort. | governance and client-value claim | supported | SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-005 | Keeps the chapter from collapsing into a cost story |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | AI will fully automate fund accounting or eliminate the need for human review. | overclaim | Keep the chapter anchored in supervised operation and bounded autonomy |
| RISK-002 | BNY already operates the complete AI-native servicing model described here. | factual overreach | Present the chapter as a disciplined synthesis of public capabilities and the target operating model |
| RISK-003 | Every servicing workflow should become autonomous at the same pace. | overgeneralization | Distinguish triage, monitoring, and recommendation from signoff-sensitive actions |
| RISK-004 | Faster processing alone is the main value of AI-native servicing. | shallow framing | Emphasize transparency, exception reduction, explainability, and control evidence alongside speed |
