## Risk Claims To Handle Carefully

| Claim ID | Risky claim | Why it is risky | Handling note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | AI can replace portfolio managers or investment committees in the near term. | Overstates evidence and weakens the chapter's credibility. | Avoid; keep focus on assisted judgment and governance. |
| RISK-002 | BNY already operates a fully AI-native investment-management stack. | Public evidence does not support that claim. | Treat the chapter as a disciplined future-state synthesis grounded in BNY direction of travel. |
| RISK-003 | Analytical abundance automatically commoditizes all active management. | Too broad and invites an unnecessary active-versus-passive detour. | Keep the argument narrower: some process layers compress faster than trust and mandate-specific judgment. |
| RISK-004 | Better models automatically create better investment outcomes. | Ignores governance, data quality, mandate context, and client trust. | Tie value to process quality, explanation, and controlled decision support rather than performance promises. |
