## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Pershing is just a custody platform. | misleading | Keep clearing, settlement, reporting, workflow, and service layers visible |
| RISK-002 | Official Pershing marketing proves internal advisor pain or fragmentation levels. | weak evidence | Add practitioner sources before making strong friction claims |
| RISK-003 | Platform status automatically means high switching costs or superior economics in every segment. | overgeneralization | Describe leverage carefully and avoid broad universals |
| RISK-004 | Wove or Pershing X already proves a fully intelligent advisor platform today. | premature | Treat current releases as signals of direction, not proof of full-state transformation |
| RISK-005 | Market-wide advisor-friction evidence automatically proves Pershing-specific client pain. | overreach | Use third-party workflow evidence to support the broader chapter frame, but distinguish market-level pressure from directly evidenced Pershing-specific shortcomings |
