## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Treasury services are just payment processing. | misleading | Keep liquidity, controls, and exception management visible in every section |
| RISK-002 | Public materials prove exact BNY internal fragmentation in treasury operations. | weak evidence | Frame internal burden as careful inference unless stronger evidence is added |
| RISK-003 | All payment rails and timing models work the same way across markets. | overgeneralization | Qualify by geography, rail, and instrument context |
| RISK-004 | Faster payment movement always means lower risk and lower cost. | misleading | Explain tradeoffs in funding, controls, and exception handling |
