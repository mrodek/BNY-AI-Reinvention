# Claim Register: CH14

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | BNY already has much of the platform surface required for intelligence because it operates across data, records, workflows, controls, and client touchpoints. | strategic synthesis | supported | SRC-001, SRC-003, SRC-007 | Inherits CH12 platform breadth and adds AI-adoption context |
| CLM-002 | The missing layer is not another product but a governed intelligence layer that can assemble context, interpret events, prioritize work, and support decisions across the platform. | conceptual framing | supported | SRC-002, SRC-005, SRC-006, SRC-007 | Defined as a strategic and operating requirement, not a literal current-state product claim |
| CLM-003 | AI is strategically more valuable in regulated financial infrastructure when embedded in workflows and unique institutional data than when used only as isolated productivity tooling. | strategic interpretation | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-004 | Keep the contrast disciplined; do not dismiss productivity gains entirely |
| CLM-004 | Financial-services AI adoption is constrained by governance, data quality, privacy, security, and trust requirements, not just by access to powerful models. | factual synthesis | supported | SRC-005, SRC-006 | Core regulated-finance qualification |
| CLM-005 | The intelligence opportunity is to improve context assembly, routing, explanation, and decision support while preserving human oversight and auditability. | strategic synthesis | supported | SRC-002, SRC-005, SRC-006 | Important to keep this from drifting into full-autonomy rhetoric |
| CLM-006 | Once intelligence is defined as governed contextual understanding, the next bottleneck becomes shared meaning across systems, which is why CH15 follows. | structural synthesis | supported | SRC-006, SRC-007 | Bridge claim into ontology chapter |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | BNY lacks AI capability today. | false binary | The chapter should argue that BNY has AI activity and platform ingredients, but not yet the full cross-platform intelligence layer implied by the thesis |
| RISK-002 | Intelligence means removing humans from financial operations. | overstatement | Keep the emphasis on governed decision support, context, routing, and evidence |
| RISK-003 | Model access alone will create durable advantage. | weak strategy claim | Tie advantage to proprietary context, workflow position, governance, and trust |
| RISK-004 | A single centralized data pool is always the answer. | architectural overreach | Use Project Aikya and BIS constraints to show why collaboration, privacy, and control complicate naive centralization |
