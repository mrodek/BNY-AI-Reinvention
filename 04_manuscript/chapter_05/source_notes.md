## Drafting Notes

- Keep the chapter operator-first and concrete.
- The strongest spine is:
  1. define asset servicing as an operating stack, not a narrow product
  2. explain why clients pay for it and why it is economically durable
  3. walk through custody, accounting, reporting, investor servicing, and oversight as one connected process
  4. make the burden visible through reconciliations, corrections, controls, and exception handling
  5. close by showing why this business is a prime target for later AI-native redesign

- Keep the business-model discussion high level:
  - recurring, trust-sensitive, operationally embedded revenue
  - do not invent segment margins or staffing mix

- Keep the workflow-burden language precise:
  - supported: controls, reconciliations, corrections, exception handling, standardized workflows, account-level data exchange
  - inference only: exact BNY internal fragmentation or labor intensity

- Avoid turning the chapter into a product inventory.
- Avoid previewing CH20 too heavily; the close should only establish transformation potential.
- For the deeper draft, make each layer explicit:
  1. custody and settlement support
  2. books and records / investment accounting
  3. pricing, valuation, and NAV production
  4. fund administration, reporting, and governance
  5. investor servicing and transfer agency
  6. corporate actions and distributions
  7. the reconciliation and exception-management fabric across all of them
