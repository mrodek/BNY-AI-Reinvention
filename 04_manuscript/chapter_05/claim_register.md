# Claim Register: CH05

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | Asset servicing is more than safekeeping; it combines custody, fund accounting, administration, reporting, and investor servicing into one operating stack. | descriptive synthesis | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003 | Core definitional claim for the chapter |
| CLM-002 | Asset servicing is a strong business because clients value trust, accuracy, compliance, reporting, and continuity in recurring workflows. | strategic interpretation | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-004 | Strong support from BNY + OCC framing |
| CLM-003 | The polished client surface of asset servicing sits on top of a workflow stack that still depends on controls, oversight, standardized workflows, reconciliation, corrections, and exception management. | interpretive | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-005, SRC-008, SRC-011, SRC-013, SRC-014, SRC-015 | Now supported directly at the process level; still avoid claiming direct proof of BNY-specific internal fragmentation |
| CLM-004 | Fund accounting makes the business operationally deeper than plain custody because it requires ongoing valuation, NAV oversight, reporting, and lifecycle administration. | descriptive interpretation | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002 | Important distinction for the chapter |
| CLM-005 | Asset servicing runs through an operational process stack that includes trade processing, settlement support, reconciliations, account administration, transfer activity, reporting, and account-level data exchange. | descriptive synthesis | supported | SRC-008, SRC-009, SRC-010, SRC-011, SRC-012, SRC-013 | Useful chapter-spine claim for explaining how the business actually operates |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Asset servicing is mostly manual. | overstatement | Say exception-heavy, control-intensive, or coordination-heavy instead |
| RISK-002 | Public materials prove internal fragmentation directly. | weak evidence | Frame fragmentation as a careful inference from emphasis on unification, workflow standardization, and reduced manual effort |
| RISK-003 | Custody and fund accounting are identical businesses. | misleading | Keep the distinction clear while showing how they connect |
| RISK-004 | Public materials prove exact segment economics or labor mix. | weak evidence | Keep the economics discussion high level unless a stronger public source base is added |
