## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | AI will soon replace portfolio managers outright. | overstatement | Keep the chapter focused on augmentation, workflow change, and shifting advantage rather than full replacement claims |
| RISK-002 | Active-management differentiation disappears wherever AI is available. | overgeneralization | Distinguish between commoditized analysis and mandate-specific judgment, governance, process, and client trust |
| RISK-003 | BNY's public materials prove a complete front-office investment-management platform in the same way Pershing proves a wealth platform. | weak evidence | Be precise about where BNY is strongest: data, analytics, accounting, operations, and lifecycle support |
| RISK-004 | Automation pressure alone explains asset-management economics. | reductionist | Include fees, scale, governance, client trust, and product structure where relevant |
