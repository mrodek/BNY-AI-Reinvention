# Source Notes

## Source Mapping

### SRC-001

- Source: BNY "What We Do"
- Role in chapter: supports BNY's claim to operate across the financial lifecycle
- Key usable points:
  - BNY says it assists clients in creating, administering, managing, transacting, distributing, and optimizing assets.
  - Useful for lifecycle framing, not just company description.

### SRC-002 / SRC-003 / SRC-004

- Source: DTCC and Investor.gov lifecycle/settlement explainers
- Role in chapter: support the high-level lifecycle model from trade to settlement
- Key usable points:
  - Settlement is the final step of a securities transaction.
  - The lifecycle is a sequence, not a single event.
  - These sources help explain the machine in plain English.

### SRC-005

- Source: BNY Fund Accounting / Fund Services
- Role in chapter: supports the servicing layer after assets are booked
- Key usable points:
  - Fund accounting includes NAV, oversight, reporting, and operational workflows.
  - Useful for showing that the machine continues after trading and settlement.

### SRC-006

- Source: BNY Global Payments & Trade
- Role in chapter: supports the liquidity and cash-movement layer
- Key usable points:
  - BNY states it handles large-scale daily payment volumes.
  - Useful for showing that asset movement and money movement are linked.

### SRC-007

- Source: BNY Global Clearing Services & Custody Expertise
- Role in chapter: supports the integrated post-trade framing
- Key usable points:
  - BNY describes integrated clearing and custody services across the post-trade lifecycle.
  - Helps tie settlement, custody, and financing together.

### SRC-008

- Source: BNY Investment Lifecycle Management
- Role in chapter: supports the end-to-end lifecycle framing
- Key usable points:
  - BNY says it supports every stage of the investment journey.
  - Strong source for the idea that the firm sits across the machine rather than in one silo.

### SRC-009

- Source: BNY Global Collateral Management Platform
- Role in chapter: supports collateral as part of the connected machine
- Key usable points:
  - BNY reports managing $7.4 trillion in collateral.
  - Supports the idea that financing and collateral are part of the lifecycle, not side businesses.

### SRC-010

- Source: BNY homepage scale statements
- Role in chapter: supports scale and breadth
- Key usable points:
  - BNY says it touches approximately 20 percent of the world's investable assets.
  - BNY says it provides custody services in over 100 markets.

### SRC-011

- Source: BNY Pershing clearing and settlement
- Role in chapter: supports front-to-back processing language
- Key usable points:
  - Useful for showing that processing and custody can be delivered as connected services.

### SRC-012

- Source: Federal Reserve Board FMU overview
- Role in chapter: supports why these infrastructure functions matter systemically
- Key usable points:
  - Payment, clearing, and settlement activities are essential financial infrastructure.

### SRC-013 / SRC-014

- Source: BNY Integrated Fund and Investor Solutions / Integrated Fund Services
- Role in chapter: supports the servicing-and-administration layer
- Key usable points:
  - BNY presents fund accounting, administration, investor services, and data as connected operating capabilities.
  - Useful for showing that the machine continues through administration and reporting, not only transaction completion.

### SRC-015

- Source: State Street investment servicing overview
- Role in chapter: external language for asset servicing and lifecycle connectivity
- Key usable points:
  - Investment servicing is useful as a peer term for integrated servicing, administration, reporting, and data support.
