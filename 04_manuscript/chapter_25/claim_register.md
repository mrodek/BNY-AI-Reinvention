## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | BNY already operates the full enterprise workflow model described in CH25. | factual overreach | Present the chapter as a disciplined target operating model built from public direction and workflow evidence |
| RISK-002 | KYC is the only hard part of onboarding. | shallow framing | Keep the chapter explicitly focused on post-KYC steps such as documents, tax forms, account setup, permissions, and activation |
| RISK-003 | Proxy voting should become fully autonomous. | control overreach | Emphasize policies, disclosures, client instructions, exceptions, and reporting as supervision-sensitive elements |
| RISK-004 | A copilot on top of existing systems is sufficient to make these workflows AI-native. | weak architecture framing | Keep the chapter focused on shared context, controlled execution, and enterprise observability |
