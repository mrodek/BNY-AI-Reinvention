## Working Hypotheses

- AI-native Pershing should be framed as a context-rich wealth operating system rather than as a custody platform with an assistant attached.
- The strongest early gains are in advisor workflow coherence, onboarding and service orchestration, investor transparency, and multi-custodial data usability.
- Pershing becomes unusually strategic because intelligence is visible to advisors and clients, not only to internal operators.
- Bounded autonomy belongs in task assembly, next-best action support, document and workflow routing, search, explanation, and low-risk self-service guidance, while advice, approvals, and consequential account actions remain more supervised.
- Wove matters because it turns interoperability from a back-end integration topic into the front-end operating model.

## Source Selection Notes

- Official Pershing and Wove sources were prioritized because the chapter depends on what currently exists in the platform and how Pershing is officially positioning those surfaces.
- CH08's source base was explicitly carried forward so the chapter can show a disciplined before/after rather than re-argue why Pershing is a platform.
- Wove sources were included because CH22 is the right place to move beyond current-state platform logic into the visible intelligence layer across advisory, investor, data, and connect surfaces.
- The chapter intentionally avoids leaning on generic AI-in-wealth commentary because the more important question is how the platform changes operationally, not whether AI is popular in wealth management.
- Evidence gap: public sources are strong on platform capabilities and workflow direction, but weaker on specific production metrics for AI-native outcomes. The chapter should present the future state as a disciplined synthesis from disclosed capabilities, not as an internal implementation reveal.

## Themes Emerging

- The best future-state frame is not "advisor copilot." It is "wealth operating system with shared context."
- Pershing's visible workbench and client surfaces make AI value unusually legible compared with more buried infrastructure businesses.
- Interoperability matters because connected data and connected tasks are what let a workflow keep context across applications and custodians.
- Multi-custodial capability changes the strategic ceiling because Pershing can become an intelligence layer across a broader wealth workflow, not just its own custody book.
- Client and advisor experience improve when the platform can explain state, not just display data.

## Gaps Resolved

## Research Pass 1 Findings

- CH08 remains the right current-state baseline. Pershing is already a real platform business, but the remaining burden sits in context assembly, integration friction, and user-side workflow stitching.^[SRC-001]
- NetX360+ is still an important bridge source because it describes an end-to-end platform for trading, settlement, custody, and account management, with connectivity to hundreds of third-party providers through Wove Connect and machine-learning-assisted workflow support.^[SRC-002]
- The Advisor Experience page makes the user-visible shift concrete: client-centric views, streamlined onboarding, machine-learning-assisted search, and analytics are already being used to improve how advisors navigate the day.^[SRC-003]
- The Operations Experience page is equally important because it shows rules-driven processing, real-time alert workflows, work baskets, document management, and service-center coordination. That means CH22 can frame the future-state platform as intelligence for both advisors and operators, not just advisors.^[SRC-004]
- NetXInvestor and Wove Investor make the client surface part of the chapter, not an afterthought. Pershing is clearly moving toward more self-service, digital authorization, consolidated documents, and multi-custodial client views.^[SRC-005]^[SRC-008]
- Wove itself is best understood as the visible future-state operating layer. The platform page and the Wove Advisory page explicitly frame the product around connected tools, common alerts, integrated search, workflow systems, intelligent account management, and multi-custodial trading and reporting.^[SRC-006]^[SRC-007]
- Wove Connect and Wove Data are what keep this from collapsing into UX rhetoric. They make explicit the roles of APIs, single-source-of-truth data, micro front ends, normalized data, analytics-ready delivery, and AI/ML-enabled data workflows.^[SRC-009]^[SRC-010]
- BNY's 2025 annual report supports the larger enterprise direction around interoperability, embedded controls, and AI throughout products and processes, which helps position CH22 as a visible expression of the broader thesis rather than as a disconnected wealth-tech bet.^[SRC-011]
