# Claim Register: CH22

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | AI-native Pershing should be framed as a context-rich wealth operating system rather than as a custody platform with an assistant attached. | strategic framing | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-006, SRC-007, SRC-010 | Core guardrail for the chapter |
| CLM-002 | The clearest early gains in AI-native Pershing are better advisor workflow coherence, smarter onboarding and service orchestration, richer investor self-service, and more usable multi-custodial context. | workflow claim | supported | SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-007, SRC-008, SRC-009 | Main operating-model claim |
| CLM-003 | Interoperability and normalized data are prerequisites for useful intelligence in the Pershing platform because workflows span many applications, roles, and often multiple custodians. | architecture-to-business synthesis | supported | SRC-001, SRC-006, SRC-007, SRC-009, SRC-010, SRC-011 | Direct bridge from Part IV into CH22 |
| CLM-004 | Human supervision should remain strongest where workflows touch advice, approvals, client-authority questions, consequential account actions, or custody-sensitive controls, even as lower-consequence routing and explanation become more autonomous. | control-boundary claim | supported | SRC-003, SRC-004, SRC-005, SRC-007, SRC-011 | Important to avoid overclaiming autonomy |
| CLM-005 | Pershing is one of BNY's clearest visible AI-native proving grounds because intelligence can improve workflow quality for advisors, operators, managers, and end clients at the same time. | strategic and platform claim | supported | SRC-001, SRC-002, SRC-006, SRC-008, SRC-010, SRC-011 | Keeps the chapter tied to the broader book thesis |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | Pershing already operates the full AI-native wealth operating system described in the chapter. | factual overreach | Present the chapter as a disciplined synthesis from current platform surfaces and disclosed direction |
| RISK-002 | Advisors can or should delegate fiduciary judgment and final advice decisions to the platform. | overclaim | Keep the chapter focused on workflow intelligence, context, and execution support rather than autonomous advice |
| RISK-003 | Better interfaces alone are the main source of value. | shallow framing | Emphasize shared context, workflow continuity, and operating coherence |
| RISK-004 | Multi-custodial capability automatically eliminates fragmentation. | overgeneralization | Make clear that multi-custodial scale raises the value of better intelligence but does not solve coherence by itself |
