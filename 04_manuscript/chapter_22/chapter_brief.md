## Chapter Purpose

CH08 argued that Pershing is one of BNY's clearest existing platform businesses because other firms already run important parts of their operating model on top of its custody, clearing, workflow, reporting, and advisor-facing surfaces. The platform is commercially strong now, but it still leaves too much context assembly, workflow stitching, and queue management to humans.

CH22 should show how that platform changes when intelligence is embedded into the operating surface itself. The target state is not "an advisor chatbot." It is a more coherent wealth operating system in which advisor workbenches become client-state cockpits, onboarding and service operations become more anticipatory, investor self-service becomes more guided, and the multi-custodial data layer becomes usable enough to support next-best actions rather than just better screens.

## Key Reader Questions

- What would an AI-native Pershing platform actually feel like for advisors, operations teams, and end clients?
- Which parts of the current Pershing stack change most when intelligence is embedded into the workflow itself?
- How do NetX360+, Wove Advisory, Wove Connect, Wove Data, and client-facing experiences fit together in a future-state operating model?
- Where does bounded autonomy help in wealth workflows, and where should explicit human control remain?
- Why does visible workflow intelligence make Pershing strategically different from a custody platform with nicer interfaces?

## Scope

### Out of scope

## Desired Reader Outcome

The reader should finish CH22 believing that an AI-native Pershing platform would make intelligence visible in a way many other BNY businesses cannot. Advisors would spend less time reconstructing client state across systems. Operations teams would spend less time moving low-information work through queues. Clients would get a clearer and more unified operating experience. Management would get a better line of sight into productivity, service friction, and workflow quality across the platform.

The chapter should also make Pershing feel strategically important beyond wealth custody. It should read as one of the clearest cases where BNY can turn an existing platform into an intelligence surface.

## Structural Guidance

Suggested sequence:

1. Open from CH08's key tension: Pershing is already a platform, but not yet a fully intelligent one.
2. State the AI-native shift: from connected tools to a context-rich operating system.
3. Walk through the main layers that change: advisor workbench, onboarding and service operations, investor experience, and data/connect infrastructure.
4. Include at least one compact advisor or client journey example before abstracting upward.
5. Distinguish bounded autonomy from advice, approval, and control-sensitive steps that still need explicit human ownership.
6. Close on why Pershing is one of BNY's most visible AI-native proving grounds.
