# Appendix A: Pershing Product Inventory

## Purpose

This appendix is a reference map of Pershing-branded platforms, solution families, and adjacent offerings that matter for understanding the Pershing stack. It is designed to support CH08 and later platform chapters without forcing the chapter prose itself to become a product catalog.

Current as of: `2026-04-09`

## How To Read This Appendix

- `Core to CH08`: central to the current-state platform chapter
- `Useful context`: helpful for understanding the broader Pershing stack but not essential to the main narrative
- `More relevant to CH22`: important for the future-state platform and intelligence chapter
- `Needs caution`: branding or scope is real, but the current public description is too broad, adjacent, or dynamic to lean on heavily in the chapter prose

## Client-Type View

| Client type | Most relevant Pershing offerings | Why they matter | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent broker-dealers | `Clearing, Custody and Settlement`, `NetX360+`, `Advisor Experience`, `Operations Experience`, `Retirement Solutions` | These firms need the full regulated operating base plus advisor and operations workflow support | Start with carrying/custody/account infrastructure, then move outward to workstation and servicing layers |
| RIAs and hybrid advisors | `NetX360+`, `Advisor Experience`, `Operations Experience`, `Advisory Solutions`, `Managed360`, `Investment Solutions` | The platform matters as both custodial base and workflow surface for advisory business management | This is where the integration and workflow-friction story is most visible |
| Bank-trust and wealth firms | `Bank and Brokerage Custody`, `Clearing, Custody and Settlement`, `NetX360+`, `Investment Solutions`, `Retirement Solutions` | These firms often span multiple account and custody models, which makes Pershing's unified experience framing more important | Useful when speaking to firms with both brokerage and trust-style operating complexity |
| Home-office / enterprise wealth platforms | `NetX360+`, `Operations Experience`, `Advisory Solutions`, `Wove`, `Data Solutions` | The value here is workflow control, supervisory visibility, and the ability to standardize the advisor environment across many users | Good client type for discussing operating leverage rather than only advisor convenience |
| Managed-account program operators | `Managed360`, `Advisory Solutions`, `NetX360+`, `Operations Experience` | These users care about proposal, onboarding, account maintenance, reporting, strategist/model access, and service continuity | Useful when the conversation centers on scale, onboarding friction, and lifecycle operations |
| Advisors serving HNW / complex households | `Bank and Brokerage Custody`, `Investment Solutions`, `Alternative Investment Solutions`, `BNY Precision Direct Indexing`, `Wove Advisory` | These offerings matter where the platform supports more complex account structures, alternatives access, tax-aware workflows, and differentiated portfolio delivery | Some of these are more future-state or adjacent, so separate today's platform from tomorrow's differentiated advisory experience |
| Retirement-focused advisors and firms | `Retirement Solutions`, `Retirement Plan Network`, `NetX360+` | Retirement adds account-type breadth, servicing variation, and workflow differences that extend the platform beyond standard brokerage accounts | Helpful when mapping Pershing's breadth across business lines, but not a central CH08 narrative layer |
| Investors / end clients | `Wove`, `Advisor Experience` | Public materials increasingly signal investor-facing and advisor-facing workflow visibility rather than purely back-office delivery | More relevant to CH22 and later user-experience discussions than to CH08's core current-state explanation |
| Institutional / hedge-fund style clients | `Prime Brokerage Solutions`, `Trading Services` | These are part of the broader Pershing/BNY offering set, but sit outside the main wealth-infrastructure thesis of CH08 | Keep separate from the chapter's core Pershing story unless explicitly needed |
