## Purpose

This appendix should function as a manuscript-grade reference map of Pershing-branded platforms, solution families, and adjacent offerings. Its job is to help readers and operators decode the Pershing stack without forcing CH08 itself to become a brochure.

It should also serve as an FDE-friendly orientation artifact: useful for account preparation, platform understanding, and later commercial or transformation discussions.

## Reader Need

- What are the main Pershing-branded platforms and solution families?
- Which offerings matter most to the current-state CH08 platform story?
- Which offerings are more relevant to CH22 and later future-state platform discussion?
- How should an operator think about the Pershing stack by workflow layer and by client type?

## Scope

### In scope

- core Pershing platform anchors
- adjacent solution families relevant to wealth infrastructure
- client-type view of the offering set
- appendix-ready categorization of which offerings belong in CH08 versus later chapters

### Out of scope

- deep competitive comparison
- precise commercial packaging, pricing, or economics
- exhaustive institutional or non-wealth BNY product mapping

## Editorial Guidance

- Keep it reference-oriented, not promotional.
- Use concise one-line descriptions.
- Prefer classification clarity over coverage sprawl.
- Treat this as an appendix artifact that supports CH08 and CH22 rather than replacing either chapter.
