# Claim Register: CH20

## Core Claims

| Claim ID | Claim | Type | Status | Evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLM-001 | AI-native clearing and settlement should be framed as continuous settlement-readiness supervision rather than as autonomous post-trade completion. | strategic framing | supported | SRC-001, SRC-003, SRC-005, SRC-009, SRC-013 | Core guardrail for the chapter |
| CLM-002 | The clearest early gains in AI-native clearing and settlement are cleaner trade-state creation, earlier exception detection, smarter case assembly, and pre-fail intervention before breaks convert into settlement failures. | workflow claim | supported | SRC-005, SRC-006, SRC-007, SRC-008, SRC-013 | Main operating-model claim |
| CLM-003 | Better visibility into liquidity, collateral, and netting conditions becomes more valuable as settlement windows tighten and markets move toward more continuous trading. | market-structure and operating claim | supported | SRC-003, SRC-005, SRC-009, SRC-010, SRC-011, SRC-012 | Connects workflow redesign to business value |
| CLM-004 | Human supervision and formal market controls should remain strongest where workflows affect legal obligations, margin and funding decisions, final books and records, or changes to official settlement status. | control-boundary claim | supported | SRC-001, SRC-005, SRC-009, SRC-010, SRC-013 | Keeps the chapter disciplined |
| CLM-005 | AI-native clearing and settlement should improve resilience, predictability, and operational transparency, not only compress cycle times. | governance and resilience claim | supported | SRC-001, SRC-005, SRC-006, SRC-007 | Prevents the chapter from collapsing into a speed story |

## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | AI will eliminate the need for clearinghouses, central counterparties, or human-supervised post-trade controls. | overclaim | Keep the chapter anchored in infrastructure-governed workflows and bounded autonomy |
| RISK-002 | BNY or DTCC already operate the full AI-native clearing-and-settlement model described here. | factual overreach | Present the chapter as a disciplined synthesis of public capabilities and direction of travel |
| RISK-003 | Real-time settlement is always superior to netted or T+1-style models. | shallow framing | Make the netting, financing, and liquidity tradeoffs explicit |
| RISK-004 | Faster settlement by itself creates durable competitive advantage. | incomplete framing | Emphasize exception reduction, readiness quality, resilience, and capital-and-liquidity efficiency alongside speed |
