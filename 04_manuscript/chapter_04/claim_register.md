## Claims To Avoid Or Qualify

| Claim ID | Claim | Risk | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RISK-001 | BNY is impossible to replace. | overstatement | Use "hard to displace" or "costly and risky to replace" instead |
| RISK-002 | Regulation is purely a moat. | oversimplified | Present regulation as both burden and barrier |
| RISK-003 | Durability means inevitability. | misleading | Preserve room for technological and strategic change |
| RISK-004 | BNY's trusted-provider language proves switching costs by itself. | weak inference | Treat provider consolidation as evidence of client preference for resilience, not as conclusive proof of lock-in |
