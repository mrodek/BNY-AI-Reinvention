# Source Notes

## Source Mapping

### SRC-001

- Source: Investor.gov, Executing an Order
- Role in chapter: supports the opening from a familiar buy order to execution
- Key usable points:
  - A retail-adjacent investor action is a good entry point into the lifecycle.
  - Execution is a visible step, but not the end of the chain.

### SRC-002

- Source: Investor.gov, T+1 Settlement Cycle
- Role in chapter: supports settlement timing and explains what settlement means in plain English
- Key usable points:
  - Settlement is the official transfer of securities to the buyer and cash to the seller.
  - Useful for showing that the trade is not complete at execution.

### SRC-003

- Source: Investor.gov, Market Participants
- Role in chapter: supports the cast of institutions after execution
- Key usable points:
  - Useful for brokers, clearing corporations, depositories, and transfer records.

### SRC-004 / SRC-005

- Source: DTCC / NSCC overviews
- Role in chapter: support clearing, netting, and the progression from matched trade to completed settlement
- Key usable points:
  - NSCC provides clearing, netting, and guarantee of completion for equity trades.
  - DTC is where movement of securities ownership and payment completion become legible in infrastructure terms.

### SRC-006

- Source: BNY Safekeeping, Transactions & Settlements
- Role in chapter: supports BNY's place in settlement, reconciliations, safekeeping, and reporting
- Key usable points:
  - Useful for explaining where BNY becomes visible in the chain after execution.

### SRC-007

- Source: BNY What We Do
- Role in chapter: supports the broader lifecycle framing
- Key usable points:
  - BNY presents itself as operating across the full financial lifecycle.

### SRC-008

- Source: BNY Pershing Custody
- Role in chapter: supports safekeeping and custody after settlement
- Key usable points:
  - Helps explain how custody links completed trades to ongoing ownership and servicing.

### SRC-009

- Source: Federal Reserve Board FMU overview
- Role in chapter: supports why reliable post-trade infrastructure matters systemically
- Key usable points:
  - These activities are essential financial infrastructure.

### SRC-010

- Source: OCC Custody Services
- Role in chapter: supports the connection between settlement, safekeeping, and reporting
- Key usable points:
  - Custody includes settlement, safekeeping, and reporting of securities and cash.

### SRC-011 / SRC-012

- Source: Investor.gov on record-keeping and proving securities ownership
- Role in chapter: support the downstream durability of the trade in the reader's world
- Key usable points:
  - Statements, records, and ownership proof are part of how the trade becomes durable and reviewable after execution.

### SRC-013 / SRC-014

- Source: SEC adviser-custody guidance and SEC fund-custody rule release
- Role in chapter: support a careful clarification that many regulated client and fund assets are maintained with qualified or otherwise permitted custodians under prescribed conditions
- Key usable points:
  - The legal framing is qualified or permitted custody, not a blanket shorthand that all assets must always be held at an unrelated third party.
