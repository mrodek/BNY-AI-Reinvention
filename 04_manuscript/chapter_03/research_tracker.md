## Source Selection Notes

- Why these sources were chosen: this first pass prioritizes investor-facing and infrastructure sources that can support a broad-audience securities-purchase walkthrough, then layers in BNY primary sources to show where the firm sits once execution gives way to settlement, custody, reporting, and servicing
- What was intentionally excluded: generic strategic material, highly technical clearing-agency rule texts, and niche institutional scenarios that would make the chapter less broadly legible
- What still needs to be added: possibly one concise source or example that makes a light-touch exception or settlement break visible without overloading the chapter

## Themes Emerging

- Theme 1: CH03 should make the machine felt through one transaction
- Theme 2: the chapter should show that execution is only the visible beginning of the lifecycle
- Theme 3: a retail-adjacent securities purchase is a good anchor because it feels familiar while still exposing the institutional layers beneath the surface
- Theme 4: the most useful sequence is broker order routing -> trade execution -> clearing/netting -> settlement -> custody/safekeeping -> records/reporting
- Theme 5: BNY becomes most legible after execution, when the chapter moves into settlement, safekeeping, reconciliations, and reporting
- Theme 6: reporting, statements, transfer records, and ownership proof are part of how the trade becomes durable in the reader's world, not just in institutional operations

## Gaps

- Gap 2: still need to decide exactly how much DTC/NSCC detail belongs in the main prose versus notes
