### SRC-005 - Federal Reserve: The Dynamics of the U.S. Overnight Triparty Repo Market

- Full source: Federal Reserve Board, "The Dynamics of the U.S. Overnight Triparty Repo Market," August 2, 2021. https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/the-dynamics-of-the-us-overnight-triparty-repo-market-20210802.html
- Why it matters: best neutral official explainer of how triparty repo actually clears and why intraday timing matters.
- Key supporting points:
  - repos function as collateralized borrowing and depend on acceptable collateral and haircuts
  - triparty repos are settled through a clearing bank that provides valuation, margining, management, settlement support, and collateral allocation
  - overnight triparty repo has a persistent intraday clearing cycle rather than one simple batch event
  - collateral type, liquidity, and relationships affect pricing and timing
- Main draft usage:
  - supports the explanation that this is a live coordination system rather than a static pool of assets
  - grounds the chapter's treatment of timing pressure and movement feasibility

### SRC-007 - ISDA Suggested Operational Practices for Collateral Management

- Full source: ISDA, "Collateral Management Suggested Operational Practices." https://www.isda.org/2020/02/07/suggested-operational-practices/
- Why it matters: strongest practitioner source on substitutions, reconciliation, disputes, and eligible-collateral schedules.
- Key supporting points:
  - substitutions are necessary for collateral and liquidity optimization and can involve repo, securities lending, and treasury operations
  - recall and return are explicit operating events
  - dispute mitigation requires governance, reporting, technology, and regular reconciliation
  - eligible-collateral schedule management is resource-intensive and time-consuming
- Main draft usage:
  - supports the chapter's burden map around substitutions, recalls, reconciliation, and schedule logic
  - supports the claim that queue quality and recommendation quality matter
