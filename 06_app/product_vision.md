# Product Vision

The companion app should evolve from a searchable knowledge companion into an adaptive organizational coach.

## Stage 1

- Search and Q&A over approved book content
- Role-based summaries
- Daily coaching prompts

## Stage 2

- Add organizational decisions, engagements, and lessons learned
- Support pattern matching against prior cases
- Surface contextual recommendations

## Stage 3

- Personalized coaching
- Continuous learning from new work
- Feedback loops between outcomes and guidance
