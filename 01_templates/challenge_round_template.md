# Challenge Round Template

## Purpose

Use this file to pressure-test a chapter draft before moving into a more polished revision. The goal is to identify what informed insiders, skeptical practitioners, adjacent functions, or executive readers would challenge, and decide which challenges should be addressed in the next draft.

## When To Use

- Typically after `draft_v1` or `draft_v2`
- Before a chapter becomes too polished to rethink easily
- When a chapter is making important assumptions, introducing a taxonomy, or drawing strong boundaries
