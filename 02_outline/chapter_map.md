## Suggested Drafting Sequence

1. CH01-CH04 to establish credibility and the current-system frame.
2. CH11-CH19 to move quickly into diagnosis, platform reframing, and AI-native foundation.
3. CH34-CH39 to make the execution path concrete before the middle business-line reinvention chapters are fully drafted.
4. CH05-CH10 and CH20-CH25 to deepen each business line with current-state and future-state detail.
5. CH26-CH33 to complete the cross-product intelligence and market-expansion thesis.
